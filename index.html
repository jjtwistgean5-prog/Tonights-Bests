How the ranking works. Positions for the Sun, Moon and 153 deep-sky objects (all Messiers plus southern and NGC showpieces) are computed every 10 minutes through the night. For each object the planner finds the moment in full darkness when it is easiest to see, accounting for altitude and atmospheric extinction, your sky's brightness, your aperture, the object's surface brightness, and the Moon's added sky glow and distance from the object. Ratings are estimates meant for planning: transparency, seeing, filters and experience all shift what you'll actually see. Positions are J2000 and accurate to about a degree.
